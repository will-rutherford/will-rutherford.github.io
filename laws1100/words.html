How to use these pages

  1. Read the last line of the exam question ("Advise X under Tort Law / the ACL / Contract Law 3 / the law of agency"). That tells you which page to open.
  2. At the top of that page, THE ORDER box lists your booklet headings. Write them in that order.
  3. Under each heading: copy the Law box. If there's a "Pick the ONE row" table, choose the row that matches your facts. Then fill in the Application sentences with facts from the question, and finish with the Conclusion sentence.
  4. Stuck? Open the Full mini-answer at the bottom of the page to see it all done for a real past question.
