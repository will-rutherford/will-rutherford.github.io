How this pack works

Each topic page has two parts.

  1. Learn fast: the big idea, each concept in short bites with diagrams, step-by-step methods, how it's examined, traps, and a self-check. 🎓 boxes flag what you already know from QCAA. 🆕 boxes flag what's new or done differently at uni, so focus there.
  2. 🖨 Exam sheet: a dense white crib built to be looked up mid-exam. It starts with an "if the question says … → do this" table.

The Print binder puts every exam sheet in one document behind a cover page that maps clue words to tabs. Print that and bring it.

The exam is open book, so don't memorise formulas. What you need is speed at finding the right method and reading numbers off scaled grid diagrams. Most questions are dropdowns or multiple choice built on a diagram.

🎯 Drill the patterns

Pattern quiz: 59 exam-style questions on the 15 patterns that come up every paper. You get instant feedback with the decoy, and a 🔁 Missed list to clear. Do each section straight after reading its topic page.

🗂 Question bank: every question from the six modern papers, grouped by section with the exact answer. It's binder tab QB, for looking up a near-identical past question mid-exam.
